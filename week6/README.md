I was pretty stuck on a concept for a while. The one thing I knew was that I liked the idea of a clock that isn’t conventionally readable, but I couldn’t figure out how to get there.

I like doing monoline scribbles, and the idea came up by accident last week while I was prototyping for my project on Playful Interfaces for I&P I messed up an illustration and scribbled over both the writing and the drawing. One of the marks ended up looking like a face, and I honestly really liked how random that was. As brought up in the brief overview of the clock assignment, chaos could be part of a clock. And that was when it clicked: let some randomness into how time shows up, so the passing of time looks a little different every time you look at it.

For the visuals, night and day get different backgrounds to show the contrast between the two, and each has its own color palette so the lines pop against the background. But this was definitely a fun and rewarding process. Watching the lines form random squiggles was satisfying, and as the hour progresses they start to overlap and you can really see figures take shape.
