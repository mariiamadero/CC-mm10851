To initiate the process of projection mapping (and as a good refresher), I started off with a fairly simple color palette, also to depict the contrast and create a shading effect, almost, from the colors and the boxes when projected. 

I made two boxes, with my first one creating an ombre line effect going down five times. I wanted the sides facing out to almost look like they would lead into and introduce the beginning of the next ombre line effect, so I made one greyscale and one colorful. That was a bit challenging because I was unfamiliar with alpha and opacity (e.g. fade), so it took a lot of trial and error. Figuring out what patterns to use and how to get them to flow so they lined up took some time.


My second box was a bit easier. I wanted to make it look like cheese, a cheese box (I wish a giant cheese box existed because I love cheese). I just worked with noise to create the different hole shapes within a real cheese figure inside the square frame, which gave it a similar feel to cheese. I used the same sketch for the other 3 sides. I wanted the circles to move within the frame as well.
